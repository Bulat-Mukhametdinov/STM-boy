### Speaker And Audio (phone, phyphox, fan background -55 dB)

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker | 8 ohm, 0.5 W (datasheet) |
| Usable band (stepped sine, 10 cm) | ~800 Hz - 10 kHz; peaks 1.6 and 3.15 kHz; below ~500 Hz under the background |
| Saturation | analog chain stops getting louder at ~-8 dBFS |
| 0 dBFS / 200 Hz | rattle + strong speaker heating (~1.3 W clipped into 0.5 W speaker) |
| Idle hiss | audible by ear, masked by the fan in measurements (+1..2 dB only for 94/187 kHz carriers) |
| Pop | amp enabled after PWM is already at mid-scale = no pop; PWM ramp with amp on = thump. Fixed as default (`audio_amp_on(0)` precharge 30 ms), HWCAL `amp_ramp_ms=0` |

Rules for games: digital peak <= -9 dBFS, sustained tones <= -12 dBFS,
high-pass 400-500 Hz, emulate bass with harmonics.

### Input Latency

Optical measurement skipped (no photodiode; a yellow LED gave no usable
signal). `input.latency` now has no dead time and a 2 ms scope marker on PA8
(R24). Derived estimate: **~20-40 ms button-to-pixel** (DMA ~7 ms + panel scan
0-12 ms + TN response 10-20 ms), plus up to one game frame.

### Power

| State | Current | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board off (ATtiny only) | ~4 uA | ~35 mAh/year |
| Menu (BL 100 %) | ~61 mA | |
| S12 max load (BL100 + LCD DMA + CPU) | 60.5 mA | ~239 mW; load used in the battery cycle |

Current vs SYSCLK (demo ray tracer, compute-bound, BL 100 %, 16-bit):

| SYSCLK MHz | Battery mA | fps | Charge per frame (mA*s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 48 | 50.9 | 20.3 | 2.51 |
| 72 | 57.3 | 30.4 | 1.88 |
| 84 | 60.6 | 35.9 | 1.69 |
| 96 | 63.3 | 40.7 | 1.56 |
| 120 | 66.8 | 51.0 | 1.31 |
| 144 | 69.3 | 61.0 | 1.14 |

- CPU costs only **~0.19 mA per MHz**; ~43 mA is fixed (mostly backlight).
- fps scales exactly with SYSCLK.
- For compute-bound work a higher clock is more energy-efficient per frame.
  Down-clocking saves at most ~10 mA (96 -> 48 MHz); **backlight level matters
  far more** than CPU frequency for battery life.

### Battery Capacity (open, F13)

With the measured S12 power, the 9 h 18 min discharge to the 3.30 V loaded
cutoff delivered **~500 mAh / 1.88 Wh**, while the cell is labelled 310 mAh.
The cell volume (2.3 x 33 x 50 mm) makes ~490 Wh/L plausible for LiPo, so the
label may simply be conservative. To be confirmed by a logged full charge
(coulomb count of the ~175 mA CC phase).

### Fixes Today

- Pop-free amplifier sequence made the default.
- W25Q erase: 4 KB and 64 KB erases sometimes silently ignored (F4) ->
  retry when an erase "completes" in < 1 ms.
- `input.latency` dead time removed; PA8 scope marker.
